## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store in your browser or on your device. They can support website functionality, remember preferences, and help measure how visitors use a website.

Similar technologies may include browser storage, pixels, scripts, and identifiers that store or access information on your device.

The specific technologies used by this Website depend on its actual implementation and enabled services.

## 3. Categories of Cookies and Similar Technologies

### 3.1 Strictly necessary technologies

These technologies may be used when required to deliver a requested feature, maintain security, or support essential Website functionality.

Examples may include technologies needed for security or basic operation.

They should not be used to collect information for unrelated purposes. Whether a particular technology qualifies as strictly necessary depends on its actual purpose and applicable law.

### 3.2 Preference and functional storage

The Website may remember preferences such as:

- Light or dark theme.
- Language selection.
- Other interface preferences, if implemented.

These preferences may be stored using cookies, local storage, or another browser mechanism.

For example, the Website may use `localStorage` to remember a visitor's theme selection. This is browser storage rather than a cookie, although similar legal considerations may apply depending on the technology and jurisdiction.

The actual mechanism will be documented after implementation.

### 3.3 Analytics cookies

The Website may use Google Analytics or a comparable analytics service to understand how visitors use its pages and to improve navigation, content, and performance.

Depending on the configuration, analytics technologies may collect or process information such as:

- Pages viewed and interactions.
- Session and visit statistics.
- Browser and device characteristics.
- Approximate geographic information.
- Online identifiers used to distinguish visits or users.

For example, a standard Google Analytics implementation may use a first-party cookie named `_ga` to distinguish users and sessions. The cookies and identifiers used, their duration, and the information collected depend on the analytics configuration and enabled features.

Analytics is intended for audience measurement and Website improvement. Advertising or remarketing technologies are not covered as active features unless separately implemented and disclosed.

### 3.4 Advertising and marketing technologies

Advertising, remarketing, and cross-service advertising measurement are not assumed to be enabled on this Website.

If such technologies are introduced in the future, this policy must be updated to describe their purposes, providers, data practices, and applicable consent requirements before they are activated.

## 7. Cookie Duration

Cookies may be session-based, meaning they expire when the browsing session ends, or persistent, meaning they remain stored for a defined period or until deleted.

The actual duration depends on the cookie, provider, and configuration.

For example, Google documents a typical two-year lifetime for its `_ga` cookie. This is a provider-documented default, not a guarantee that every implementation uses that duration. Settings and implementation details may affect the actual behavior.

Before publication, the Website owner should inspect the deployed Website and document the actual cookies, their purposes, providers, and expiration periods.
